Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Plainware Studio apps handle data.

Apps Covered

  • Splits
  • Fasting Timer
  • Countdown Timer

1. Splits

Splits is an interval timer app by Plainware Studio.

  • Splits works offline.
  • No account is required.
  • Splits has no ads.
  • Splits has no analytics currently.
  • Splits has no third-party tracking currently.
  • Custom timers, routines, descriptions, sound settings, voice settings, imported routines, and preferences are stored locally on the user's device.
  • Imported .splits files are stored locally when the user chooses to import them. Exported .splits files are only created when the user chooses to export them.
  • Splits may show a running notification while a timer is active.
  • Splits may use Android Text-to-Speech for voice cues.
  • Splits may use vibration for alerts.
  • Splits uses foreground service functionality to keep active timers reliable in the background or on the lock screen.

2. Fasting Timer

Fasting Timer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No internet access. The app declares no INTERNET permission and makes no network requests.
  • Local storage only. All fasting sessions, weight logs, water entries, notebook entries, and settings are stored on your device only.
  • No accounts, analytics, or advertising. There is no sign-in, no crash reporting, no analytics SDK, and no advertising network.
  • Notifications are generated locally by the Android operating system. No notification content is transmitted externally.
  • Android backup. If Android's automatic backup is enabled on your device, app data may be backed up to your Google account as part of Android's standard device backup. This is controlled by your Android and Google account settings, not by this app. Backups are encrypted by Google and are not accessible to the developer.
  • Data deletion. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • Medical note. This app is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any fasting programme.

3. Countdown Timer

Countdown Timer is a countdown and count-up app by Plainware Studio.

  • Countdown Timer works offline.
  • No account is required.
  • Countdown Timer has no ads.
  • Countdown Timer has no analytics currently.
  • Countdown Timer has no third-party tracking currently.
  • Countdown Timer declares no internet permission.
  • Events, reminders, widget settings, recurrence settings, archived events, and app preferences are stored locally on the user's device.
  • Backup and restore files are only created or imported when the user chooses to export or import them.
  • Countdown Timer may show local notifications for reminders.
  • Countdown Timer may use Android widgets to display selected events on the home screen.
